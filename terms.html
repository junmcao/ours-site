1. What Ours does

Ours is a household organization and ledger tool for tracking shared responsibilities, schedules, and expenses. Ours does not move money, process payments, or provide financial or legal advice. Any settlements recorded in Ours reflect transfers you make outside the app (for example, via your bank's Interac e-Transfer or cash).

2. Your household, circles & data

When you share an invite code or link with another person, they join your household and can view and edit shared records (shared expenses, chores, work rhythms and shared busy times). When your household joins a circle, the other households in it can view and edit the circle's costs and settlements, and see your household's name and size. You are responsible for what you add and for inviting only people you trust. You can leave a household or circle, or delete your account, at any time.

Balances in Ours are calculated from what people enter. Check amounts before you pay anyone, and settle disagreements directly with the people involved.
