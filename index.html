Ours

Keeps chores off Sam's shift days, stays quiet while either of you sleeps, and tells you when you're both free.

What it does

Less asking, less keeping score

Everything is tagged as yours, theirs or both, so nobody has to remember who said they'd do it.

Chores that fit around shifts

Chores rotate between you and skip shift days and the sleep after nights. Fairness is counted over the whole rotation, not one busy week.

Split what's shared, keep the rest private

50/50 or by income, like 55/45. Spending you mark "Just mine" stays on your phone.

Settle up in your bank app

One tap opens your own bank for an Interac e-Transfer, with the amount and note ready. Ours never touches your money.

Together time

Finds the evenings you're both free, across shift patterns and your calendars. Only "busy" is shared, never the details.

Quiet while you sleep

Reminders wait until you're awake, including after a night shift. One morning summary instead of a stream of pings.

Scan it or say it

Snap a receipt, or tell Siri "dinner was $86, split it." Receipts are read on your phone and never uploaded.

Glance, don't open

Home and Lock Screen widgets. Tick off chores without opening the app, and see a countdown to the end of your partner's shift.

Your spreadsheet, kept up to date

Paste one formula into Google Sheets and your shared costs and chores stay current there, for budgets and charts. "Just mine" spending is never included.

Circles of households

A shared cottage, a family trip, two homes raising the same kids. Each household's share lands in its own Money, and nobody sees your own spending.

Getting started

Five minutes, then it runs itself

Set up together

One of you starts a household and sends a link or QR code. Partners and roommates join the same household; a circle connects a few households.

Add your rhythms

Pick a work pattern like 9 to 5 or DDNN, connect calendars if you like, and add rent or one bill.

Let it run

Chores rotate, bills remind the right person, and the balance keeps itself. You just tap done.

No ads and no bank connections. Your partner only sees what you share. You can export or delete everything from the app.
