Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Ours helps couples, roommates and families share chores, schedules and expenses, and lets households team up in circles to share costs with each other. We built Ours so that private information stays on your iPhone unless you choose to share it.

1. What stays on your iPhone

  • Personal expenses: Any expense marked Just mine (private) is stored only on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to the sync server or shared with anyone in your household or circles.
  • Receipt photos: When you scan a receipt, text recognition runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device Vision framework. Receipt images are never saved or uploaded.
  • Calendar event details: If you connect your calendars, Ours reads event times on your iPhone and shares only busy-time windows with your household. Event titles are shared only for calendars you set to share details. Locations and notes never leave your iPhone.
  • Diagnostics: Crash and performance reports from iOS are kept on your iPhone. They are only sent anywhere if you choose Send diagnostics and pick how to send them.

2. What is shared with your household

When sync is on, these records are stored in our database (hosted by Supabase in the United States) and shared only with the people in your household:

  • Display names, work rhythm patterns and quiet hours
  • The email address or phone number you enter for Interac e-Transfers, if you enter one
  • Shared expenses, splits and settlements
  • Shared chores, recurring bills and completion history
  • Calendar busy times (and event titles only for calendars you choose)

Access rules in the database let only signed-in members of your household read or write your household's records.

Like any web service, Supabase keeps short-lived technical logs of requests to the server, including IP addresses, to run and secure it. They aren't used for anything else and are deleted automatically within a few days.

3. Circles: sharing with other households

A circle is a few households sharing costs, for example a cottage or a family trip. When your household joins a circle, the other households in it can see:

  • Your household's name in the circle, which is made from your members' display names, and how many people live there
  • Costs added to the circle, which household paid, and each household's share
  • Settlements between households in the circle
  • The person your household chose to receive circle money, and their e-Transfer email or phone only if your household chooses to share it

Other households never see your household's own expenses, chores, calendar, work rhythms or anything marked Just mine. Your household's share of a circle cost is copied into your own household's records, where only your household can see it.

4. Invites and links you share

  • Invite codes and links let someone join your household, or let another household join a circle. Codes expire after 7 days and can be turned off at any time. Expired codes are deleted 30 days later.
  • Spreadsheet link: If you create a live link for Google Sheets, anyone who has that link can read your household's shared expenses, settlements and bills (never Just mine expenses). Only a fingerprint of the link is stored on our server. You can turn it off or replace it in Spreadsheet sync.

5. Feedback

If you send feedback from the app or our website, we store what you write, the name and contact details you choose to include, and the app version, so we can read it and reply. Feedback is not linked to your household's data.

6. Accounts & Sign in with Apple

You can use Ours on a single iPhone without an account, or sync across phones using an anonymous session or Sign in with Apple. Anonymous accounts that never join a household are deleted after 30 days. We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics trackers or third-party tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data.

7. Keeping, exporting & deleting your data

  • Export: At any time, go to Household settings › Account and privacy › Export my data to export spreadsheets (CSV) of your expenses, settle-ups, and chores and bills, plus a complete JSON archive of your household data.
  • Leave a household or circle: Your shared history stays for the people still there, shown as a former member, so their balances still add up. If you move out without leaving in the app, someone still in the household can mark you as moved out, which works the same way and stops your account seeing the household's new records.
  • Delete account: Go to Household settings › Account and privacy › Delete account. This revokes your Sign in with Apple token (if linked), removes your account from the sync server and wipes all local data from your iPhone. A household with nobody left in it is deleted along with its records.
  • Backups: Encrypted database backups are kept for up to 30 days, so deleted data may remain in them until they expire.

8. Children

Ours is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
